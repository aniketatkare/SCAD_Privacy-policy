Privacy Policy

App: CAD Script Studio · Package: com.tecknowinventors.scad
Developer: Tecknow Inventors · Last updated: November 8, 2026

मराठी सारांश ↓

In short: CAD Script Studio works on your device. Your scripts and projects stay on your phone. The app has no ads, no analytics, and does not sell or share your personal data.

1. About the app

CAD Script Studio lets you write OpenSCAD scripts, render 3D models, preview them, and export or share them as STL or .scad files. Rendering is performed locally on your device using a bundled OpenSCAD WebAssembly engine.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit your personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up is required to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Projects and scripts: Your .scad scripts and saved projects are stored in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Preferences: Settings such as language and 3D viewer options are saved locally.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

4. Files you import, export, and share

  • Import: When you choose to import a file, the system file picker opens and the app reads only the file you select.
  • Export/Share: When you tap STL or SCAD, the app creates a temporary file and opens the Android share sheet. You decide where the file goes. We do not receive it.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Declared so the app can show notifications if needed. You can disable this in system settings.
  • Microphone: The app includes a media component that may request microphone access, but it is not required for CAD features and is not used to record you. You can deny it without losing functionality.

6. Third-party services

The app bundles the open-source OpenSCAD WebAssembly engine (licensed under GPL-2.0), which runs entirely on your device. It does not send your data anywhere. Content you share through other apps (email, cloud storage, messaging) is handled by those apps under their own privacy policies.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

8. Data security

Because your data is stored locally, its security depends on your device's protections (screen lock, encryption, backups). We recommend keeping your device updated and locked.

9. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

10. Your choices

You can delete any project inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date revised.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: aniketatkare@gmail.com
